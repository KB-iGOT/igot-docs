# Explore Content

How a Karmayogi finds what to learn next. One search box at the top of the
portal covers the whole catalogue — courses, programs, assessments and events
— and the Explore menu offers the same catalogue as browsable categories.
Filters narrow results by competency, topic, content provider, language and
duration.

- **Where**: the top-bar search, `/app/globalsearch`, and the Explore menu
  (`/app/explore`)
- **Who**: every signed-in Karmayogi; authors and coordinators have their own
  search views

## What users get

There is nothing to configure and no path to follow: type a word or tap a
tile, and the catalogue answers. Results are one list across every content
type, so a learner never needs to know *where* content lives. Newly published
content appears in search shortly after it goes live.

## How a Karmayogi experiences it

1. **Search** — type in the top bar, or open the Explore menu
2. **Filter** — narrow by competency, provider or language
3. **Preview** — open a result to see details and ratings
4. **Start** — enrol or begin learning right from the result

For how this works underneath — the search service, the gateway path and the
index — see [HLD](hld.md) and [LLD](lld.md).
