What the code shows

CAP appears in the attached repos as a distinct ECourseCategory with three verified behaviours: it is the default deep-link target of the mandatory-notification modal (learners are pushed to it by notification), its cards are exempt from end-date display in the consumption strips, and it has its own section handling in the home-page content strips. Its consumption mechanics are those of the assessment engine documented under Standalone Assessment.

Honest gap: no CAP-specific enrolment or evaluation endpoint appears in the attached repos — the category rides the shared assessment and course rails. If there is a dedicated CAP backend (e.g. in the assessment service), attaching that repo would let this page say more. Treat this page as a stub-plus, not finished documentation.
