What it is

A Karmayogi joins a Community, posts a Question inside it, and other members answer with Answer Posts and nested Answer Post Replies — all three levels stored by one service (cb-discussion-service) in just two Postgres tables, discriminated by a type field. Reports past a configurable threshold auto-suspend the content; an MDO leader or community moderator then reviews it in the Org Portal's Community → Manage screen. A separate Kafka-driven worker asynchronously keeps each community's engagement counters in sync.

Honest gap: the platform ships two unconnected "comment thread" systems that share a confusable name — the community Q&A flow documented here is entirely separate from cb-comment-service/comment-tree-service's workflow-content-comment feature. See the HLD tab for the full naming-collision note.
Two independent CommentTree implementations

comment-tree-service is a read-only Redis-fronted cache facade over the exact same Postgres table cb-comment-service writes — coupled only by a shared table and a shared, weak, checked-in secret.

How a Karmayogi experiences it

1 · JOIN

Join a public community — private communities have no request-to-join flow at all

2 · POST

Ask a Question inside a joined community

3 · ANSWER

Others reply with Answer Posts and nested Answer Post Replies

4 · MODERATE

5 reports auto-suspend; a moderator hides/restores via the admin endpoints

5 · SYNC

Community counters update asynchronously via Kafka
