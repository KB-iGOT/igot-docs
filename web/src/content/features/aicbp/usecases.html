CBP author: step by step

Step 01 · CBP author
Log in to the platform

Open the AI CBP Tool, sign in with the credentials issued to the department and land on the dashboard.

Steps

  1. Open the iGOT AI CBP Tool in the browser.
  2. Enter the credentials issued to the department.
  3. Sign in to reach the dashboard.
Step 02 · CBP author
Select ministry / department

Choose Centre or State, then the State and the Department, and move on to Manage Documents.

Steps

  1. Pick whether the organisation is Centre or State.
  2. For a State, choose the State name, then the Department from the dropdown.
  3. Tap Manage Documents to continue.
Step 03 · CBP author
Manage relevant documents

Upload the mandatory Work Allocation Order first, add optional supporting documents, and watch each AI summary complete.

Steps

  1. Upload the mandatory Work Allocation Order first; a live badge shows progress.
  2. Add optional supporting documents such as Annual Reports or other supporting documents to improve accuracy; download the sample formats before preparing files.
  3. Use Upload Document, choose the document type, then drag and drop or browse for the PDF.
  4. Preview, download or delete any file from the table, and search by name or type.
  5. Keep to up to 10 PDFs, each under 25 MB, official, publicly available and text-searchable; avoid confidential files.
  6. Watch the AI summary status go Pending, In Progress, then Completed.
Step 04 · CBP author
Generate CBP: initial draft

The tool reads the designations in the Work Allocation Order, matches them to the iGOT Designation Master and drafts role mappings and competencies.

Steps

  1. Generate CBP reads every designation in the uploaded Work Allocation Order; without that document the action is disabled.
  2. Each designation is matched against the iGOT Designation Master; matches go to Matched Role Mapping with a live count and the rest to Unmatched Role Mapping.
  3. Roles and competencies are mapped with the KCM framework, giving the initial draft CBP.
  4. If a CBP already exists, Generate CBP opens a prompt: View Existing CBP opens the last one, Yes starts a new generation that overwrites the previous data.
  5. Treat the draft as a starting point and check both tabs.
Step 05 · CBP author
Manage unmatched role mapping

Fix designations the AI could not match by choosing the right one from the iGOT Designation Master.

Steps

  1. Open the Unmatched Role Mapping tab to see designations that were not auto-matched.
  2. Use the 3-dot menu on a row and choose Edit Role Mapping.
  3. Pick the correct designation from the iGOT Designation Master dropdown and tap Add and Update.
  4. The row moves to Matched Role Mapping and the counts update at once.
  5. Clearing unmatched rows enables View Final CBP and Send for Approval.
Step 06 · CBP author
Request a new designation in the iGOT master

If a designation does not exist in the master, send it to the SPV Admins for approval instead of forcing a match.

Steps

  1. Request a new designation only when it genuinely does not exist in the iGOT Designation Master.
  2. From Unmatched Role Mapping, select the designation and tap Send Designation Approval; SPV Admins get an automatic email.
  3. The SPV Admin approves it or rejects it with a reason.
  4. On approval the designation joins the master and its row moves to Matched Role Mapping on its own.
  5. Follow the status under Unmatched Role Mapping; an email arrives when the SPV acts.
Step 07 · CBP author
Manage designation hierarchy

Rearrange the designation hierarchy from the profile menu.

Steps

  1. Tap the profile icon at the top right.
  2. Choose Update Designation Hierarchy.
  3. Rearrange the hierarchy with the dropdowns as needed.
Step 08 · CBP author
Add new designations from the iGOT Designation Master

Add designations the AI missed; roles are generated for them too.

Steps

  1. From the role-mapping page use Add More Designation to add any designation the AI missed from the iGOT Designation Master.
  2. Fill in roles, responsibilities and details for each, and tap Save after each addition.
  3. Role mappings are generated for the newly added designations as well.
Step 09 · CBP author
Edit the CBP

Use the built-in editor to adjust designations, responsibilities, wings, divisions and competencies, then save.

Steps

  1. Edit designations, responsibilities, wings or divisions in the built-in editor.
  2. Update Behavioural and Functional competencies so they align with KCM, and fine-tune Domain competencies for the role.
  3. Tap Save CBP when done.
Step 10 · CBP author
Generate course recommendations

Get AI-suggested courses per role mapping, filter and verify them, add more from iGOT, and save the chosen courses.

Steps

  1. Open a role mapping and tap Generate Course Recommendation, or use the 3-dot menu on a row.
  2. The tool suggests best-fit courses from the iGOT catalogue for the role's competency gaps.
  3. Narrow the list with Competencies, Ratings, Language, Duration and Provider filters, or search.
  4. Open each course on the iGOT platform to review it before adding it.
  5. Use Add More Courses from iGOT for relevant courses the AI missed.
  6. Tick the chosen courses and tap Save Courses to finalise the recommendation for that role.
  7. Blend AI suggestions with hand-picked courses.
Step 11 · CBP author
Send role mappings for approval

Tick the role mappings, pick the MDO Admin or Leader, name the request and submit; a Request ID is issued at once.

Steps

  1. Tick each role mapping to submit; the checkbox appears only after its course recommendations are saved.
  2. The Send for Approval button shows a live count, for example Send for Approval (3).
  3. Choose the MDO Admin or Leader who will review, give the request a clear Request Name, and tap Submit.
  4. A unique Request ID is generated at once and the status starts as Pending Approval; note the ID.
  5. Submitted role mappings lock for editing, so check everything first.
Step 12 · CBP author
Track role mapping approval requests

Find a request by its ID, read its status badge, open it, or revoke it while it is pending.

Steps

  1. Open Approval Requests from the profile menu.
  2. Find a request by Request ID or use search and filters.
  3. Read the badge: Pending (yellow), Approved and Published (green) or Rejected (red).
  4. Tap View to see the submitted role mappings and course recommendations.
  5. Revoke a request while it is pending; this unlocks its role mappings for editing.
  6. Statuses update after the MDO acts; refresh if the latest badge is not showing.
Step 13 · CBP author
View and download the course list

See the full course list for a role mapping, remove courses, and export it as a PDF.

Steps

  1. Tap View Course Recommendation to see the full list.
  2. Remove any course with the delete icon.
  3. Export the list as a PDF with the Download button.
Step 14 · CBP author
Download combined CBP and courses

Review all role mappings with their courses and export them.

Steps

  1. Tap View Final CBP to review all role mappings with their courses.
  2. Export as a PDF, or as a spreadsheet: the guide says CSV, while the screen and the app's own help text say Download as Excel.
Step 15 · CBP author
Download in multiple languages

Pick a language in the final CBP pop-up and download the plan in it.

Steps

  1. In the View Final CBP pop-up open the Language dropdown.
  2. Pick the preferred language and download.
Step 16 · CBP author
Finalise and download the CBP

Download the final CBP and the final course recommendations as PDFs for records and official submission.

Steps

  1. Download the Final CBP with all designations and competency mappings.
  2. Download the Final Course Recommendations alongside it.
  3. Save as PDF for records and official submission, after the Competent Authority has reviewed and cleared everything.

MDO Admin / Leader

Step 17 · MDO Admin / Leader
Review role mapping approval requests (MDO Portal)

In the MDO Admin Portal, open AI CBP Requests, review the submitted role mappings and approve or reject them.

Steps

  1. Log in to the MDO Admin Portal and open AI CBP Requests from the left navigation.
  2. The dashboard lists each request with Request ID, Request Name, Submitted By, Submitted On and a live status badge; search or filter by Pending, Approved or Rejected.
  3. Tap View to open the review screen: submitted role mappings, mapped competencies and linked course recommendations side by side.
  4. Approve: pick a training plan and a target completion date and confirm; the mapping is marked Approved and Published to iGOT.
  5. Reject: enter the mandatory reason so the Nodal Officer knows what to fix.
  6. View Non Matching Designations (top right) lists the designations the author flagged as unmatched with their SPV approval status.
  7. Both actions email the Nodal Officer and update the status at once.

SPV Admin

Step 18 · SPV Admin
Approve new designation requests (SPV Portal)

In the SPV Admin Portal, review requests to add a designation and approve or reject them.

Steps

  1. Log in to the SPV Admin Portal and open Manage Designation Approval Requests.
  2. Switch between the Pending, Approved and Rejected tabs, each with a live count.
  3. Narrow the list with search, the Division/Wing filter (Apex, Directorate, District, Block, Field) or the Organisation filter.
  4. Each row shows Created On, Designation Name, Organisation, Division, Requested By and Email.
  5. Approve (green tick): the designation is added to the iGOT Designation Master and the requester's unmatched row moves to Matched Role Mapping.
  6. Reject (red cross): give a clear reason so the MDO can resubmit if needed.
  7. Only SPV Admins can act; actions are logged and the requester is emailed.

Guidance

All users
Dos and Don'ts

What to always do and never do when using the tool.

Guidance

  • Do: upload only official, publicly available PDFs under 25 MB each; guard login credentials; treat the AI draft as a starting point; blend AI-suggested courses with hand-picked ones; keep every competency within the KCM framework; save and securely store the final CBP and course recommendations; double-check before Send for Approval, because submitted role mappings lock.
  • Never: upload confidential, classified or sensitive documents; treat AI output as final without the Competent Authority's review; add competencies outside KCM without proper approval; share login credentials with colleagues or outsiders; upload files that are too large, corrupted or not text-searchable.
